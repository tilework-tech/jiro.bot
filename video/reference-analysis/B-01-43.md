## Techniques

- **Story:** The video opens with a hook that recasts AI agents as hires: "We recently hired a new teammate" becomes "4594 new teammates". It then shows people living with the product, reveals the product name, and introduces specific bots by owner and job in lower-thirds. Humans stay in frame at all times. The product is shown as something colleagues use, not as a feature tour.
- **Kinetic type over b-roll:** One persistent caption sits dead center while the footage cuts underneath it every 0.5–1 s. Edits to the caption are the plot beats: an animated strikethrough, a slot-machine roll on the phrase, a running counter (11→514→1284→2290→3502→4594), and words sliding in as the sentence re-centers. The "Meet ___" sequence swaps a name each beat, then uses a glyph scramble before landing on the brand.
- **Pacing:** Very fast. b-roll cuts about every 0.5–1.5 s. Holds are longer only for key moments: the Dock reveal (about 1.5 s), the phone content (about 2 s), and the logo lockup (about 2 s). Caption beats change every 0.5 s during the counter and Meet sections.
- **Typography:** Clean geometric sans (Inter/SF-like), white, regular/light weight, about 4–5% of frame height, no shadow box. Legibility comes from the dim, low-key footage. The brand lockup pairs regular "Meet" with semibold "Grok Bot" plus a round white mascot glyph. Lower-thirds are left-aligned, with the name line in white and the descriptor in grey/dimmer weight.
- **Product framing:** There are no device mockups or screen recordings. Every UI shot is filmed through a camera on real hardware: macro Dock, phone in hand, over-the-shoulder laptop. These shots use shallow DOF, angled perspective, a handheld drift, and a hand or finger in the foreground. Only a sliver of UI is in focus, enough to read one meaningful message. That makes it feel authentic and premium rather than demo-ish.
- **Making it clear what we're looking at:** The Dock tooltip "Grok Bot" names the app before the logo appears. Real, mundane, specific content (desk assignments, Salesforce Okta login, "outbounded today") signals real work. The lower-third ties a bot name to a person and a quantified job.
- **Color/grade:** Warm skin tones and wood against a teal-green highlight cast, matching the product's teal UI accent. Low key, film-like, with an anamorphic-ish crop.
- **Personality:** Mascot blob avatars and playful bot names (Yodo, Han Sodo, Odo-bi-Wan, "I'll keep watch. Rest easy.") add warmth and humor.
- **Cursor:** Only the in-scene macOS cursor (Dock hover/click) appears. No synthetic cursor or highlight boxes.
- **No narration is visible** (it may be music-driven). All copy is on-screen captions.
