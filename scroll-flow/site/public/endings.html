Where does the belt end?

Five ways the conveyor could finish at the bottom of jiro.bot. Each one would replace the plain end of the belt under the footer. It would animate in a loop, react when you poke it, and use the real plates that just rode down the whole site. Click any image to enlarge. Tell me the number you want built.

1

The Koi Gulp

The belt juts out over a moonlit pond. A koi the size of a car surfaces and swallows every plate.

On the site: plates slide off the end and splash; the koi burps a bubble every 10th plate. Throw a plate at it and it does a backflip. Leave the footer idle and it winks.

serene · funny
2

Sushi Orbit

The belt turns into a rooftop ski jump. Plates launch past the moon and join a ring of sushi satellites.

On the site: the ring keeps growing with every plate you've seen ride down, so it's a quiet counter of your visit. Click the moon and Jiro zooms his telescope in on one stray tamago.

epic · whimsical
3

Final Code Review

Jiro sits at the end in a green eyeshade and stamps every plate. Good ones get a check. Slop drops through a trapdoor into a bin that burps smoke.

On the site: the bin is the anti-slop punchline. Any plate you exploded or threw earlier comes back misshapen and gets rejected. A small tally reads "shipped vs. sent back".

on-message · deadpan
4

The Kaiten Portal

The belt dives into a glowing portal. The same plates pop out of a second portal back at the top of the site.

On the site: this is the only ending that makes the belt a true loop. Scroll into the portal and the camera follows a plate through it, landing you back on the hero with no page jump.

mind-bending · interactive
5

Last Train Out

The belt feeds a tiny shinkansen. Each plate takes a window seat while station-master Jiro blows the whistle.

On the site: the train departs every 12 seconds with a whoosh and a new one glides in. Poke Jiro and he holds the doors for "one more plate". The departure board lists your open tickets as destinations.

cute · absurd
