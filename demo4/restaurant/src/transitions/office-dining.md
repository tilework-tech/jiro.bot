# office → dining

**Route:** eye level all the way, no rotation. The belt keeps running straight down the left lane (x = 150): out of the office's sushi lift through a copper collar in the office floor, down the crawl space under it (joists, a copper pipe, the sleeping **ceiling cat**, a clickable egg), through a copper collar in the dining room's ceiling boards + beam, and down the dining room's corner post into the dining IN port (top edge, x = 150).

**Camera:** one tall world column: office frame at y 0..1080, the cutaway band (`public/art/tr/office-dining/band.webp`, 472 px, built together with the dining art so its ceiling planks run seamlessly into the dining frame) at 1080..1552, the dining frame at 1552..2632. The camera glides straight down (cosine-eased, no rotation, no pitch) with a gentle 8 % dolly-in toward the lane mid-way, and lands exactly on the dining frame at t = 0.8 (the dining DOM fades in over 0.8..0.98; the dining scene paints canvas stand-ins of the two compare windows so nothing pops). t ≤ 0 draws the office scene, t ≥ 0.8 the dining scene, both unmodified.

**Belt continuity:** path A = the office belt extended from y 1080 down to the split (phase `PHI_O - LO`, key `office`), path B = the dining belt extended back up from its first point to the split (phase `PHI_D + LB`, key `dining`). For any plate `u = head - PLATE_GAP * id`, so these phases keep every plate's position and id identical across the office/A and B/dining joins. Y_OFF is chosen so `Y_OFF - 40 ≡ 0 (mod PLATE_GAP)`, so the office lattice continues straight into the dining lattice (a DEV-only check warns if the phases ever change). The item/rim swap between office and dining identity happens inside the ceiling collar, which covers a full plate sprite. All motion comes from `api.drawBelt` (beltTime), so plates move down at the constant belt speed.

**Length:** 1.6 viewport heights. Cat sprite: `office-dining/cat.ts`. Art build: `.local/jiro/dining-art/build.py`.
