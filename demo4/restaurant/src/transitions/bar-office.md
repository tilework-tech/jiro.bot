# bar → office

**Route:** the hero trough leaves the bottom edge of the full-bleed bar picture and keeps running diagonally down-left across the dark page, narrowing from the hero's perspective size to the lane's. It banks round a 110 px fillet into the left lane (x = 150), then drops through a copper hatch in the bar floor. Below the floor it rides a glass-and-copper lift shaft through the crawlspace, where small soot sprites gather round a thimble by candlelight (clickable egg `tr-floor-mice`). It enters the office ceiling as the office's sushi lift.

**Camera:** it glides straight down one tall world column: bar frame at y 0, dark page, crawlspace at `Y_B`, office frame at `Y_OFF = Y_B + 816`. The move is cosine-eased with zero speed at both ends and sits on the exact office frame from t = 0.9, while the office DOM fades in. On the way it zooms in by up to 12 % and drifts toward the left lane. It banks about 1.7° into the diagonal-to-vertical bend, and pitches slightly (vertical squash) while dropping through the floor. The parallax layers are faint far specks over the dark page (depth 0.6) and a near cross beam plus sifting sawdust in the crawlspace (depths 1.4 and 1.2). The hero's ambient animation keeps running because the bar scene is drawn live under the camera, with image smoothing off so every layer stays on its hard pixel grid. As the camera leaves it, the picture's bottom sinks into the page colour (`heroFx.exit` in bar.ts, 0 at t = 0), so there is no hard picture edge; the bar redraws its trough (the same raster as the art) over that fade so the belt itself stays lit.

**Belt:** the hero belt is one straight line fitted to the art (`src/scenes/bar/art.json`: centre x(y), half-width(y)); its plate scale grows with the painted width to `EXIT_S` = 1.65 at the bottom edge and is held there to `BED_END` = 1140. One path W runs from the bar belt's tail (`BELT_TAIL.a` at y 900, bar world distance `BELT_TAIL.u`) to `office.belt.pts[0]`. `Y_B` is nudged so `BELT_TAIL.u + |W|` is a whole number of `PLATE_GAP`s, which puts the plates exactly on the bar's plates and on the office's. W holds `EXIT_S` until y 1150, then eases it to 1 by the start of the bend. Section A (hero trough, `bar` item identity) runs from the tail to the middle of the floor; while the transition runs, the bar scene draws only its plates above the tail (`BELT_TOP`), so no plate is drawn twice. Section B (office lift, `office` identity, phase = distance to the office top) runs from there down. The item swap and the trough-to-lift style swap both happen hidden inside the floorboards. The trough uses the bar's cross-section (`TROUGH_BANDS` in REF units = horizontal px at the bottom edge, `REF_K` = 1; bands past `FRONT` are the front face, which shrinks as the belt turns; the hard contact `SHADOW` beside it) and starts at y 1040, overlapping the bottom rows of the picture so there is no seam. Its perpendicular scale follows the belt scale from the hero's (about 0.58 px per band unit) to 0.42 in the lane. The moving slat seams are the bar's (`SEAM_STEP` 24, same phase by belt distance, 3 px cells).
