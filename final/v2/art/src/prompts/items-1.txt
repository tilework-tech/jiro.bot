Row 3: onigiri with a happy face, onigiri with a sleepy face (closed eyes, tiny zzz bubble shape), onigiri with a grumpy face, a blob of wasabi with suspicious side-eye. Row 4: a ball of rice with a tiny paper cocktail umbrella, a gyoza tucked in a tiny blanket, a small dish of edamame, a tiny bowl of miso soup. Faces are simple 2-pixel dot eyes and a tiny mouth line. Warm, appetising colours. Flat pure #00FF00 green background everywhere, no grid lines, no plates. 16-bit SNES pixel art, crisp square pixels, no anti-aliasing, no gradients, no text.
