Warm lantern amber and candlelight create local pools, while copper/rust and muted salmon color the restaurant and characters; cyan eyes and green chart markers are tiny accents. Text is crisp and modern, with blocky pixel display headings and clear sans-serif body copy. The comparison and FAQ are two-column layouts with copy on the left and detailed scene art on the right.
- The screen scrolls vertically in one fixed front-on observer angle. There is no orbital camera, tilt, zoom, or abrupt scene cut in this clip. Dark timber cavities between rooms provide a linear visual breath before the next room enters.
