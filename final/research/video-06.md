## Motion and interaction reading

Browser, gallery header, and fixed site header remain in place. No camera rotation, zoom, perspective pivot, new belt, or dissolve is visible. Belt plates flow downward on the right vertical section, around a rounded right-angle corner, and left across the pond. Once the scroll stops, belt items keep moving. Clicking a decorative lantern increments an Easter egg tally and raises a themed toast. The `FLAPPY KOI` control is visible but never clicked, so its game rules, quality, and interaction state cannot be inferred from this video. There is no evidence of plate dragging or objects placed on a flat surface in this clip.

Inference for final design brief: retain the calm straight-down camera journey, dark text-safe pond surface, bridge and amber lantern contrast, continuous belt, and koi endpoint, while correcting demo-specific mismatches. The final belt should be genuinely one stream with stable spacing, realistic turns and occlusion. It should remain half-filled per Martin's current brief, not the packed demo stream. Every dish should sit on a white plate with a faint white/blue rim, including absurd items. The koi mouth should intersect the food convincingly and the waterline should occlude its body cleanly. Consider the lantern's small toast as an example of a restrained discoverable Easter egg, not as license for large overlays. The current `1/88` counter is observed in this demo; the new target is more than 50 distinct Easter eggs, so the denominator should follow the final implemented total rather than copying 88 by default.
