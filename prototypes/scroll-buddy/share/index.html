Jiro, the little bubble, on jiro.bot

Jiro lives in a corner of the page while you scroll. He hops between sides, perches on one heading, looks at what he's about to talk about, thinks, then says one line per section. He never repeats himself unless you click him.

The rules

WhatBehaviour
IdleLayered game-style hover: bob, drift, tilt and squash at different speeds, shadow shrinks as he rises. Cursor blinks. A2 C1
Once onlyEach section gets one comment per visit. Scrolling back stays quiet. Clicking Jiro replays the current one.
CalmOne trick at a time, never stacked. At most 3 side changes and 1 perch per page.
Off switch× hides him for the rest of the tab session.
Reduced motionNo hover, flights or typing. He moves instantly and text appears at once.
PhonesSmaller, stays bottom-left, only talks when tapped.
/ For whoever builds the real page

Two files and some attributes

Add jiro-buddy.css and jiro-buddy.js (deferred). Then tag sections:

AttributeDoes
data-jiro-commentHis line for this section (required).
data-jiro-ask + data-jiro-actionTurns the line into a question with a Yes button that scrolls to or focuses the selector.
data-jiro-lookSelector he looks at and highlights before speaking.
data-jiro-perchHeading he hops onto (first one wins).
data-jiro-sideleft / right corner for this section.
data-jiro-moodeager adds a double-hop before speaking.
