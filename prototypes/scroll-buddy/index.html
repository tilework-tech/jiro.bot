/ FAQ
What is Jiro

An autonomous software engineer you call into a conversation. He takes on a task, raises concerns early, and hands back a pull request for review.

How do I call him in

Mention @jirobot in a Slack thread or Linear issue and describe what you need in plain words.

Does he merge without review

No. Jiro opens a pull request and waits for a human to review and merge it.

What does he have access to

Only the repositories and integrations your team connects, inside an isolated cloud session.

Which coding agents can I run

Claude Code, Codex, and Gemini CLI, on the model subscription you already pay for.

How does billing work

A flat monthly price per developer or per team; model usage stays on your own subscription with no markup.

Why cloud agents

A cloud session keeps working while your laptop is closed and gives every task a clean, reproducible machine.

Who makes Jiro

The Nori team, who build agent-first developer tooling.
