Head + antenna expressions: what Reachy teaches the bubble

The 25 tiles are all the real V3 bubble with the same face throughout. Every expression comes from the head and the two free ends of the outline at the top gap, which act as Jiro's antennas. Star what you like, add notes, hit Save.

Key frames from Cliff's Reachy video (@darkseed, 32 s). Captured with Playwright, one frame every 0.5 s: 0–8 s · 8–16 s · 16–24 s · 24–32 s

What I see in the 65 frames

  • The face never changes. Two fixed lens "eyes" for 32 s. Everything readable comes from pose.
  • The antennas move independently: both straight up = attentive (2.0 s, 18 s), one tipped out = curious (1.5 s, 9 s), splayed into a V = open/relaxed (16.5 s, 28.5 s), both leaning the same way = following the head (21 s).
  • Head tilt (roll), about ±10°, appears in nearly every frame. It's the "dog listening" read.
  • Head turn and nod point the eyes at a person or thing. The body barely moves.
  • Rising up off the body (6.5 s, neck visible) = perking up. It's the biggest single move in the clip.
  • It's never fully still. Small, slow, continuous drift between poses.
Caveat: at 0.5 s per frame, fast whips are invisible. The springy overshoot of the antennas (row C2, A5) is inferred from the coil springs at their base, not seen frame by frame.
