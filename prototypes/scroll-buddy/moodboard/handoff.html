The per-pass budget

A pass is one scroll in one direction. A new pass starts when you reverse direction by at least 60px. The budget resets every pass.

≤ 1 big movethe edge peek, once per page visit, desktop only
≤ 2 small movesscoot 7px or hop 5px, at least 2s apart
≤ 1 lineabout the section he sits on; never a section he already commented on
≤ 1 bouncy hoverspring or eager; otherwise game idle or sleepy
Rest ≥ 2sbefore he follows to the next section

After 4 long passes (each covering at least half the page) he stops talking until someone clicks him. Clicking always gets a line and resets the quiet counter.

Where Jiro can and cannot appear

The live rules from spotFor(), shown on a schematic desktop section. He starts at each section's anchor marker, then moves to the nearest clear spot if the anchor is unsafe.

CENTRE BAND 35–65% (desktop)
HEADINGS, TEXT, BUTTONS
EMPTY SPACE BESIDE HEADINGS
SOLID-COLOUR AREAS
DOCK INTO JIRO LOGOS
  • Where he should rest: empty monochrome space beside headings, solid-colour areas, and the outer bands (left or right 35%) on desktop. On the Ditch and FAQ sections he docks into the existing Jiro logo and replaces it while he sits there.
  • Never on top of: headings (h1–h3, the hardest rule), body text, labels, links, buttons, inputs, list items or table cells.
  • Never in the centre band (35–65% of the width) on screens 768px and wider.
  • Never off-screen at rest: he keeps at least 8px from the viewport edges. The only time he is partly off-screen is the edge peek.
  • Phones (under 768px): a 56px mascot, no centre-band rule and no edge peek. The same rules against covering text and headings apply. The FAQ uses its own phone anchor.
  • After you drag him: exactly where you dropped him, fixed on screen, until the tab is closed. Kept inside the viewport. While dropped he does not travel, peek or dock.

Comment spots

Comments live on the page, not in the code. A section opts in with data-jiro-comment="…". Adding data-jiro-anchor sets where he rests in it, and data-jiro-ask plus data-jiro-action add Yes/Later buttons. These are the 8 sections on the mockup today. Any other section with a comment attribute becomes a spot.
