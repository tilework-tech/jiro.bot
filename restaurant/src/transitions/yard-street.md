# yard → street: "Down the roof, along the gutter"

**Route:** the yard belt keeps going down the left lane, through the gap in the hedge and a gap in the mossy garden wall, then down the wet kawara roof of the shop next door. At the eave it turns right into the copper rain gutter and rides it to a copper hopper head stencilled "SUSHI ONLY". It comes out the bottom as the downpipe on the shop front, passes the upstairs neighbour's window (Mr. Tanaka, pyjamas, newspaper, the odd "!!") and becomes the street's drainpipe belt.

**Camera (length 1.5):** it is one continuous world in yard stage space. The street frame sits at (900, 2160), and `public/art/tr/yard-street/panel.jpg` (2988×1680) is drawn at world (0, 760) under both frames. A Catmull-Rom camera follows the belt: it dollies in to ×1.3 and goes down through the hedge and wall onto the roof (t 0–0.3), with rain starting after the wall. It banks right (+1.6°) around the eave corner, pans along the gutter at ×1.4 while pink, cyan and violet neon haze rises out of the canyon (0.3–0.6), then banks the other way (−1.3°) down the pipe and pulls back to the street framing (0.6–1). The near rain layer has vertical parallax. Frame edges that face the panel are feathered: the yard's bottom edge by 200 px and the street's top and left edges by 320 px. Near the ends the camera snaps to whole pixels, so t=0 and t=1 match the scenes exactly.

**Belt continuity:** leg A uses the yard's own start point, phase and key (`yard`), so it covers the yard belt exactly and continues it. Leg B starts inside the hopper and uses key `street` with phase = its length to `street.belt.pts[0]`, so its plates, items and seams are the street belt's own. Plates swap identity out of sight inside the hopper head.
