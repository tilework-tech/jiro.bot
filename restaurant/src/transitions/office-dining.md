# office → dining

**Route:** the sushi lift keeps dropping down the office's left wall (x = 150), through the office floor, which is cut open like a dollhouse. It passes the crawl space (joists, a lost chopstick, and the sleeping **ceiling cat**, a clickable egg), goes through a copper collar in the dining room's big ceiling beam, drops straight through a giant red paper lantern ("JIRO") where the plates show as shadows, runs down the dining back wall past a STAFF ONLY plate, and bends onto the floor into the dark service corridor (the dining IN port, top edge x = 150).

**Camera (the big 3D move):** a real pinhole camera that only pitches about the x axis. The world is two textured planes: the WALL (Z = 0, the office frame plus the canvas-drawn floor slab, beam and dining back wall) and the FLOOR (Y = YF, the live dining frame, top-down, image y toward the viewer). Pitch-only means each texture row maps to one screen row, so each plane is drawn as horizontal strips (`drawPlane`) into a CPU-backed frame canvas, then banked in one blit. The camera slides down the lift (0.06–0.78), dollies ~330 px toward the lane and back, pitches 0° → 90° (0.22–0.90) while its target moves out over the floor, and banks up to 2° over the corner. At t ≤ 0.03 / ≥ 0.97 it draws the real scene frames. The projection is identity at both rest poses, so there is no pop (canvas diff vs the scenes: 0 px at the ends, AA-only differences on the belt stroke at 0.035 and 0.96).

**Belt continuity:** path A = office belt extended to YMID (key `office`), path B = dining belt extended backwards up the wall from the corner to YMID (key `dining`). YF is snapped (≡ 40 mod PLATE_GAP for the current phases) so plates hand over exactly; the item swap happens behind the ceiling beam. Plates never stop and keep BELT_SPEED in world units.

**Length:** 1.6 viewport heights. Art lives in `office-dining/wall.ts` (all canvas-drawn on the 3 px grid; no image files).
