# storage → yard: "Over the edge, under the floor"

**Route:** the storage belt's bottom run turns down at x=150 and drops through the floor trapdoor. The camera tips over the edge of the storage floor with it, so we are looking straight down into the crawlspace under the building: footing stones, a lost geta, a spilt rice sack, a sake bottle, and a tanuki asleep on a zabuton (it snores, and it is clickable: egg `tr-tanuki`). The belt runs on low trestles from the warm spill of the trapdoor into cool moonlight. It ducks under the back wall's sill through a rubber flap curtain whose strips kick as each plate passes, then comes out of the lit dish-return hatch at the top of the night yard.

**Camera:** the world is a vertical stack sharing x: storage (y 0–1080, eye level), crawlspace `public/art/tr/storage-yard/crawl.jpg` (1080–2160, top-down), yard (2160–3240). The storage wall tilts away as the camera nears the hinge, and the ground plane goes from strongly foreshortened to flat. This is a real pitch: the camera rolls over the edge and looks down. The camera also pans down along the belt with a slight push-in (×1.12), a small bank as it rolls over, and a lean toward the lane. The light shifts from the warm bulb glow to a cool moonlight wash. **Length:** 1.5 viewport heights.
